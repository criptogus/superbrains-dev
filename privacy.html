Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

SuperBrains ("we", "us") provides AI demand-forecasting tools for small businesses. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. SuperBrains is operated by ZeroAgency.

Data we collect

  • Account data — your email address, used to send login links and receipts.
  • Forecast data — the sales figures and files you upload, plus the forecasts generated from them, stored so you can revisit them in your dashboard.
  • Billing data — payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Usage data — basic, privacy-friendly pageview and feature events (no cookies, no fingerprinting, no third-party trackers).

How we use it

  • To generate forecasts you request.
  • To operate your account, process subscriptions and send transactional email.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the product.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use your uploaded data to train models — yours or anyone else's.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete individual forecasts from your dashboard at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, email contact@zeroagency.ai and we will remove it within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS) and data is stored on access-controlled infrastructure. Login uses single-use, expiring magic links — we never store passwords.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and LGPD), you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Contact us at contact@zeroagency.ai to exercise them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new "last updated" date.
