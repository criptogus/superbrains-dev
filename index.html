    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    How much sales history do I need?

    Three months of daily or weekly data is enough for useful forecasts. More history = sharper forecasts, but don't let "not enough data" be the reason you don't start.

    Is my data safe?

    Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, never shared with third parties, and never used to train models. You can export or delete everything at any time.

    What formats can I upload?

    CSV or Excel with at least two columns: a date and a value. Exports from Shopify, Square, Lightspeed, WooCommerce and most POS systems work out of the box.

    What if my business is seasonal or event-driven?

    That's exactly what the engine is built for. It detects weekly, monthly and yearly cycles plus holiday effects automatically — and the Pro plan lets you mark your own events (a festival, a pop-up, a promo).

    Do I need to know anything about AI or statistics?

    No. If you can upload a spreadsheet, you can use SuperBrains. The output is written in plain language: what will happen, when, and what to do about it.
